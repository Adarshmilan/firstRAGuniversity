# Marwadi University Examination Rules and Academic Policies

## Attendance Rules
- Minimum 75% attendance is required in each subject to appear in the end-semester exam.
- Students with 65–74% attendance may be allowed only with a valid medical certificate and HOD approval.
- Below 65% attendance: Detained; must repeat the subject.

## Examination Pattern
- Mid-semester exam: 30 marks
- Internal assessment (assignments, quizzes, attendance): 20 marks
- End-semester exam: 50 marks (conducted by the university)
- Passing criteria: Minimum 40% in each subject (internal + external combined).

## Re-evaluation and Supplementary Exams
- Re-evaluation application within 10 days of result declaration. Fee: INR 500 per subject.
- Supplementary (backlog) exams are held in July every year. Fee: INR 1,000 per subject.

## Code of Conduct
- ID card must be worn at all times on campus.
- Mobile phones are not allowed in classrooms and exam halls.
- Dress code: Formal dress on Mondays and during placement drives.
- Ragging is a criminal offence and leads to immediate suspension.
