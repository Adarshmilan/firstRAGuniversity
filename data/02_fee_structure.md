## Fee Payment Rules
- Fees can be paid in two installments: 60% before 15 July and 40% before 15 December.
- Late fee: INR 100 per day after the due date, maximum INR 5,000.
- Payment modes: Online via student portal (UPI, net banking, card), Demand Draft in favour of "Marwadi University", or at the Accounts Section counter.
- Cash payment is NOT accepted above INR 20,000.
- Fee receipts are available on the student portal within 48 hours of payment.

## Scholarships and Concessions
- Merit Scholarship: 50% tuition waiver for students with 95%+ in 12th or top 100 rank in entrance exam.
- Merit Scholarship: 25% tuition waiver for students with 90–94.99% in 12th.
- Continuation: Student must maintain CGPA 8.5 or above to keep the scholarship.
- Sibling Concession: 10% tuition waiver for the second child studying at Marwadi University.
- Government scholarships (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) are handled by the Scholarship Cell, Admin Block, Room A-104.

## Refund Policy
- Withdrawal before classes start: Full refund except INR 1,000 processing fee.
- Withdrawal within 15 days of classes starting: 80% refund of tuition fee.
- Withdrawal after 15 days: No refund of tuition fee; caution deposit is refunded.
