# Marwadi University Admission Process 2026-27

## B.Tech Admission Eligibility
- Passed 12th with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- Minimum 60% aggregate in PCM (55% for SC/ST/OBC).
- Valid score in JEE Main or State Engineering Entrance Exam.

## M.Tech Admission Eligibility
- B.Tech/B.E. in relevant branch with minimum 60% (or CGPA 6.5).
- Valid GATE score preferred; otherwise college entrance test.

## MBA Admission Eligibility
- Any graduation with minimum 50% marks.
- Valid CAT / MAT / CMAT score, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

## MCA Admission Eligibility
- BCA / B.Sc (CS/IT) / any graduation with Mathematics at 12th or graduation level, minimum 50%.

## Admission Steps
1. Fill the online application form on www.marwadiuniversity.ac.in/admissions (Application fee: INR 1,000).
2. Upload documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, Aadhaar card, passport photo, category certificate (if any).
3. Attend counselling / document verification at the Admission Cell.
4. Pay first installment of fees to confirm the seat.
5. Collect ID card and student portal login from the Admin Office.

## Important Admission Dates
- Application opens: 1 May 2026
- Last date to apply: 30 June 2026
- Counselling: 5 – 20 July 2026
- Classes begin: 1 August 2026
