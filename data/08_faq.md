# Marwadi University Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the total B.Tech fee for one year at Marwadi University?
A: The B.Tech fee is INR 1,18,000 per year. In the first year, INR 10,000 admission fee and INR 5,000 refundable caution deposit are added, so the first-year total is INR 1,33,000.

Q: Can I pay the fees in installments?
A: Yes. 60% must be paid before 15 July and the remaining 40% before 15 December.

Q: Where is the CSE HOD cabin?
A: The CSE HOD, Dr. Anjali Sharma, sits in Block A, 3rd Floor, Room A-301.

Q: When can I meet the CSE HOD?
A: Dr. Anjali Sharma is free on Monday 2:35–4:00 PM, Wednesday 11:00 AM–12:50 PM, and Friday 1:40–3:30 PM. She is not available on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

Q: What time does college start and end?
A: Classes run from 9:00 AM to 4:25 PM, Monday to Friday. On Saturday, classes are from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. 2nd and 4th Saturdays are holidays.

Q: What are the library timings?
A: The Central Library is open 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, and 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM on Sunday (reading room only). During exams it stays open till 10:00 PM.

Q: How much attendance is required?
A: Minimum 75% in each subject.

Q: Who do I contact for a bonafide certificate?
A: Apply at the Admin Office, Admin Block, Room A-003. It is ready in 2 working days and is free.

Q: Is there a hostel facility for girls?
A: Yes. The Girls Hostel is in the south campus near Block D, with 400 seats. Warden: Mrs. Pooja Saxena (0731-2400302).

Q: What is the late fee penalty?
A: INR 100 per day after the due date, up to a maximum of INR 5,000.

Q: Where is the placement cell?
A: Training & Placement Cell is in Admin Block, 1st Floor, Room A-110. TPO: Mr. Amit Bansal.
