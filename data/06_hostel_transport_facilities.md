## Hostel Rules
- Night-out permission requires a parent's written consent and warden approval.
- Visitors allowed only on Sundays, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM, in the visitor lounge.
- Wi-Fi available in all hostel rooms (40 Mbps per student).
- Ragging is strictly prohibited; anti-ragging helpline: 1800-180-5522.

- Buses leave campus at 4:35 PM (Mon–Fri) and 1:10 PM (Saturday).
- Bus pass is collected from the Transport Office, Admin Block, Room A-008.
- Transport In-charge: Mr. Dinesh Chouhan (Phone: 0731-2400120)
