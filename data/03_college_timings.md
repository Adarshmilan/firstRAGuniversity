# Marwadi University Timings and Schedule

## Regular Class Timings
- Working Days: Monday to Saturday
- Saturday: Half day (classes till 1:00 PM). 2nd and 4th Saturday are holidays.
- Sunday: Holiday

## Daily Lecture Schedule (Monday to Friday)
| Period         | Time               |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Period 1       | 9:00 AM – 9:55 AM  |
| Period 2       | 9:55 AM – 10:50 AM |
| Short Break    | 10:50 AM – 11:00 AM|
| Period 3       | 11:00 AM – 11:55 AM|
| Period 4       | 11:55 AM – 12:50 PM|
| Lunch Break    | 12:50 PM – 1:40 PM |
| Period 5       | 1:40 PM – 2:35 PM  |
| Period 6       | 2:35 PM – 3:30 PM  |
| Period 7 (Lab) | 3:30 PM – 4:25 PM  |

## Saturday Schedule
- Period 1 to Period 4: 9:00 AM – 12:50 PM
- No lunch break; college closes at 1:00 PM.

## Office Timings
- Admin Office: 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM (Mon–Fri), 9:30 AM – 1:00 PM (Sat)
- Accounts Section: 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM (Mon–Fri), 9:30 AM – 1:00 PM (Sat)
- Exam Cell: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Mon–Fri)
- Admission Cell: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Mon–Sat during admission season, June to August)

## Facility Timings
- Central Library: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Mon–Sat); 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Sunday, reading room only)
- Library during exams: Open till 10:00 PM
- Canteen: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Mon–Sat)
- Computer Center (Block A, 2nd Floor): 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM
- Gym (Sports Complex): 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- Medical Room (Admin Block, Ground Floor): 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Nurse on duty; Doctor visits 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM on Mon, Wed, Fri

## Academic Calendar 2026-27 (Important Dates)
- Odd Semester classes begin: 1 August 2026
- Mid-semester exams (Odd): 21 – 26 September 2026
- Diwali break: 6 – 12 November 2026
- End-semester exams (Odd): 1 – 15 December 2026
- Winter break: 16 December 2026 – 1 January 2027
- Even Semester classes begin: 4 January 2027
- Annual Tech Fest "Techno-Rise": 12 – 14 February 2027
- Mid-semester exams (Even): 22 – 27 February 2027
- End-semester exams (Even): 3 – 18 May 2027
- Summer vacation: 20 May – 31 July 2027
