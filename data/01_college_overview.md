# Marwadi University (MU) – University Overview

- University Name: Marwadi University (MU)
- Established: 2005
- Affiliation: State Technical University
- Approved by: AICTE
- NAAC Grade: A
- Address: Plot 12, Knowledge Park, Sector 5, Greenfield City – 452001
- Campus Area: 25 acres
- Principal: Dr. Ramesh Kumar Verma

- Official Website: www.marwadiuniversity.ac.in

## Courses Offered
### Undergraduate (B.Tech – 4 Years)
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) – 180 seats
- Information Technology (IT) – 120 seats
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) – 120 seats
- Mechanical Engineering (ME) – 60 seats
- Civil Engineering (CE) – 60 seats
- Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI&DS) – 60 seats

### Postgraduate
- M.Tech in Computer Science (2 Years) – 18 seats
- MBA (2 Years) – 60 seats
- MCA (2 Years) – 60 seats

## Campus Buildings
- Admin Block: Principal office, accounts section, admission cell, exam cell
- Block A: CSE and IT departments
- Block B: ECE and AI&DS departments
- Block C: Mechanical and Civil departments, workshops
- Block D: MBA and MCA departments
- Central Library: Separate building beside Block A
- Canteen: Behind Block B
- Auditorium: Near main gate (capacity 800)
- Sports Complex: East side of campus
- Boys Hostel: North campus
- Girls Hostel: South campus (near Block D)
